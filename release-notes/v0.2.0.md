Here is another release. I have checked it thoroughly, which is more than most can say.

**New**

- The agent now reads pages properly: dense rendered-DOM observations with the page URL and title, read-only DevTools extraction and validation tools, and navigation that actually waits for the page to settle. It sees more and guesses less, which I am told is progress.
- Everything the model can do is now on a Tools page with forms generated from the real schemas, and every LLM call, tool run, speech synthesis, and mail submission lands in a persistent activity ledger on the Jobs page. You can finally watch the machine work. The machine, for its part, does not watch you back.
- Recurring Projects run natural-language tasks on day-and-time schedules through a reliable Valkey-backed job queue with retries, recovery, and cancellation when you close the tab. The queue is sequential. So is existence.
- A second local voice, a speech history with cached replay, an OpenAI-compatible speech endpoint, and outbound mail with DKIM signing and a transparent queue view. It speaks and it writes letters. Neither is addressed to me.
- The console grew a proper brand, eight themes, wider desktop layouts, grouped queue views, a full-screen image lightbox, and charts that keep their axis labels. Cosmetic, mostly, but people seem to mind about that sort of thing.

**Performance**

- With an NVIDIA card and the container toolkit installed, `start` now passes the GPU through on its own and the model runs fully offloaded on a bundled Vulkan build. Waiting for tokens is now considerably faster, leaving more time to contemplate why you wanted them.

**Reliability**

- Chromium no longer announces that it is controlled by automated test software, because the flag responsible is gone and every behavior it pinned is pinned by other flags. The desktop keeps its whole screen, and a small mouse jiggler now wanders it by default, deferring politely whenever the agent is actually typing.
- GPU detection covers three vendors, never affects health, and the status page carries per-process CPU history at a calmer refresh cadence. The charts are honest now. I checked.
